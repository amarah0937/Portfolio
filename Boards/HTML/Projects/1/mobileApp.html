UAT Mobile App

UAT's demographic is made up of people who are fairly tech-savvy. UAT's demographic is mostly made up of people who are fairly young. Most students don't have hindering issues with technology. Most students had issues with what to enter into the log-in during the first week. A username and password example as well as a reminder to save the user's password somewhere would be useful. https://www.figma.com/design/A2gdgmT0hmEO3DML2R1RTN/UAT-Mobile-App?node-id=0-1&t=5yEm0f8ENNwuskMD-0 https://www.figma.com/proto/A2gdgmT0hmEO3DML2R1RTN/UAT-Mobile-App?node-id=1-7&t=Rrl9EzfBClhNO83L-1&scaling=min-zoom&content-scaling=fixed&page-id=0%3A1&starting-point-node-id=1%3A7 As I got started at UAT, I watched students sign into their accounts on the computers. I watched them try different ways to get in. I watched as some students got rejected because someone was already logged in. And I watched as some students got locked out because they put their password in wrong too many times. I also noticed that some students would log in with uat.edu while some students didn't. Results and Conclusion The app isn’t immediately self-explanatory but is easy to get used to. Was the test successful? Yes Design recommendations Add back button markings. Move the text box labels from below the text boxes to above the text boxes. Make the text boxes functional Having users test your prototype is great for spotting things that the developer themselves might overlook or might be used to: you knew where the search bar was so never considered visibility being a problem, but two users who didn't know the layout of your prototype had issues locating it. I think this is a great method for catching the things that developers might not catch as problems.
